A clique is a set of pairwise adjacent vertices, and an independent set (or stable set) is a

Deﬁnition 2.5. A graph is r-partite if its vertex set can be partitioned into r independent sets,
some of which may be empty. A 2-partite graph is bipartite. If V(G) ⇤X t Y is a partition
into independent sets, then (X, Y) is a bipartition, and we may call G an X, Y-bigraph.

For nonnegative integers a and b, the complete bipartite graph Ka,b has a bipartition
(X, Y) with |X| ⇤a and |Y| ⇤b, and it contains all ab possible edges between X and Y.
The graph K1,b is called a star; the vertex in its singleton part is its center, and the b vertices
in the other part are its leaves.
